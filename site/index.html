Bottleneck Engine

Why are you losing FPS?

Instead of a list of tweaks, you get one explained diagnosis. The engine samples real telemetry every two seconds while you play and names what is actually holding the game back.

  1. Six collectorsCPU, GPU, VRAM, RAM, disk and processes, read from Windows and the driver.
  2. Seven analyzersEach one votes for a cause. No single signal decides anything alone.
  3. Root cause firstIf heat or the power limit explains a "GPU limited" reading, the symptom is turned down and the cause leads.
  4. Confidence, not guessesOnly causes with real confidence are shown, at most three, each with the numbers behind it.

FAQ

Straight answers

Something missing? Ask in the RESET Discord.

How many FPS will I gain?

We don't know, and neither does anyone who promises a number before seeing your PC. The modules remove real overhead like background recording, power saving and network throttling. How much that is worth depends on what limits your game, and the Bottleneck Engine tells you exactly that.

Is it safe? Can I go back?

Every module shows its risk level before you apply it, and the previous value is backed up first. You can restore a single change or a whole session, and create a Windows restore point on top. Temp file cleanup is the only action that can't be undone, and it says so.

Can it get me banned by anti-cheat?

The optimizer changes Windows and driver settings, not games. FrameBoost doesn't inject a DLL, hook the game or read its memory; it reads the game window the way screen capture does. We can't speak for every anti-cheat, so if you're unsure, check your game's rules.

Does it work with AMD or Intel graphics?

Yes. All Windows modules work on any GPU. The NVIDIA Max Performance module and the GPU temperature readout are NVIDIA-only, and the app hides what doesn't apply to your hardware.

Why does it sometimes need admin rights?

Five modules write system-wide settings that Windows only lets administrators change. They are marked upfront, and the app offers to restart elevated instead of failing silently. Everything else runs without admin.

How do I set up a game for FrameBoost?

Set the game to borderless window, because exclusive fullscreen doesn't always work. Cap it at half your monitor's refresh rate and leave the GPU some room. The app shows the exact cap for the monitor you pick.

Does the app collect data?

No telemetry. An account is only needed for Premium and stores your e-mail address. The update check asks GitHub whether a newer release exists.

Which languages does the app speak?

English, German and Russian. You can switch live in the settings, without a restart.
